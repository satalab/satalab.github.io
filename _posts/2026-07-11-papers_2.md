We're excited to share that one of our works, “`FMiner`: CPU-FPGA Heterogeneous Framework for Frequent Itemset Mining and Beyond,” has been accepted to the ['IEEE/ACM International Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD)'](https://iccad.com/2026)!

Once again, our group proves that CPU-FPGA heterogeneous computing platforms have become a gold standard for maximizing accelerator efficacy by overcoming inherent bottlenecks and applying our novel application-driven cross-layer system architectural solutions to another new application domain: data mining. 

Huge congratulations to our first author, `Esmerald Aliaj` at UC Irvine, and sincere thanks to my esteemed advisor, Professor `Sang Woo Jun`, for his invaluable guidance and support.😄

We are pleased to announce that **Sang-Jun Park**, an undergraduate intern at **SATA Lab**, has received the **Best Paper Award** in the Undergraduate/Junior Paper Competition at the **2026 Korea Computer Congress (KCC 2026)**, organized by the Korean Institute of Information Scientists and Engineers.

His award-winning paper, **“Runtime Rule Lifecycle Management Framework for 100GbE FPGA-Based Network Packet Inspection Systems,”** presents an FPGA-based framework that treats packet-inspection rules as runtime data, enabling rule installation, activation, query, update, deactivation, and rollback without FPGA resynthesis or partial reconfiguration. 
Its on-chip double-bank rule store allows a new rule version to be validated and activated atomically while the current rule version continues to serve the packet-inspection datapath. 

The prototype was implemented on an **AMD/Xilinx Alveo U50** and configured to support **64K normalized rules**. 
It achieves an FPGA-side image-loading time of **11.4 ms** and an atomic rule-activation latency of approximately **15 μs**, while the management logic consumes only **1.18% of LUTs and 0.83% of registers**.  

We are extremely proud of Sang-Jun’s dedication, persistence, and growth throughout this research. As his advisor, it is especially rewarding to see his efforts recognized through this achievement.

**Congratulations, Sang-Jun! We look forward to his continued contributions and future accomplishments in the field of network systems architecture.**
